GARAGE KNOWLEDGE & SUPPORT

Need advice on a repair or inspection?

Ask our workshop specialists a specific technical question about your car, or browse common servicing and repair FAQs below.

How long does a full pre-purchase inspection take?
Inspections
Most pre-purchase inspections take about 90 to 120 minutes, covering computer diagnostics, underbody & suspension checks, fluids, braking, and a road test, with a clear written report shared right away.
What should I do if my check engine, ABS, or hybrid warning light comes on?
Diagnostics
Book a computer diagnostic scan before replacing parts by guesswork. If the light is flashing or the vehicle shakes or loses power, avoid highway driving and request a mobile mechanic check.
How often should I service my car on Kenyan roads?
Servicing
For normal urban and highway driving, engine oil and filter changes are recommended every 5,000–7,500 km (or every 6 months), with full multi-point servicing and brake/suspension checks every 10,000–15,000 km.
Can 3 Pedals Garage diagnose noisy suspension, steering knocks, or uneven tyre wear?
Repairs & Alignment
Yes. We inspect lower arm bushes, ball joints, shocks, tie-rod ends, and wheel bearings, then pair mechanical repairs with computerised wheel alignment and balancing.
Do you offer mobile mechanics if my vehicle won’t start or breaks down?
Mobile Mechanic
Yes. Use the Mobile mechanic tab above to share your exact location pin and issue for battery, starter, breakdown diagnosis, or on-site pre-purchase checks.
Can I bring my own spare parts or ask you to source genuine OEM parts?
Parts & Warranty
We can source verified OEM and trusted aftermarket parts for you with transparent pricing ahead of work, or fit quality customer-supplied parts once checked for compatibility.
