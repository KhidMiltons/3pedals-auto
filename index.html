FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions on inspections, selling & booking.

Clear answers on how we inspect cars before purchase, how private owners and dealers list vehicles, and how to book an inspection or workshop slot.

What is included in a 3 Pedals pre-purchase vehicle inspection?
Inspections
Every pre-purchase inspection covers a full OBD-II computer scan, engine and transmission health check, suspension and underbody assessment, braking and tyre measurement, body-paint/accident signs, and a road test — followed by a clear written report before you pay the seller.
Can I have an inspection carried out at a car yard or seller’s location?
Inspections
Yes. You can bring the vehicle to 3 Pedals Garage or request a mobile inspection at Nairobi car yards and private sellers’ locations via our Roadside & Mobile Mechanic team.
How do I list and sell my car on 3 Pedals Auto KE?
Selling
Sign in, open Sell your car, add your make, model, mileage, price in KES, up to 15 photos, and your WhatsApp number. Listings are reviewed quickly and go live so buyers can contact you directly without hidden middlemen.
Can dealerships and car yards register multiple vehicles?
Selling
Yes. Create an Organization / dealership account during sign-up to get a dedicated public yard profile page, display your yard logo and location, and manage your entire stock from one dashboard.
How do I book an inspection or garage service appointment?
Booking
Tap Book an inspection to reach our enquiry form right away, or visit 3 Pedals Garage booking to pick an open Monday–Saturday time slot (8:00 am – 6:00 pm) and receive a booking reference ticket.
How quickly do you confirm bookings and inspection reports?
Booking
Appointment slots are confirmed personally by phone or WhatsApp the same working day, and standard pre-purchase inspection reports are delivered within 2 hours of inspecting the vehicle.
