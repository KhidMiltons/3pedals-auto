GOOD TO KNOW

A few helpful answers.

When do I pay for my booking?

You submit a request first. After our team reviews availability and approves it, we confirm the quotation and provide the M-Pesa payment option. No payment is taken when you submit the initial request.

Is my booking confirmed as soon as I submit it?

No. Submission means your request has been received for review. Your booking is confirmed only after the team approves it and shares the next steps.

Can I request an airport transfer or a long-distance trip?

Yes. Choose Airport transfer or Distance-based journey and provide the pickup, destination, timing and any relevant flight or route details.

Can I request extra stops or a return journey?

Yes. Include the stops, return details and preferred timing in the special requests field so the team can consider them when preparing your quotation.

When will I receive my chauffeur's details?

Chauffeur assignment details are shared after the booking has been approved and the team has assigned a driver.

Can I change or cancel a request?

Please contact 3 Pedals Auto KE with your booking reference as soon as possible. Any change depends on availability and the booking status.

Will I receive updates on WhatsApp?

You can optionally consent to WhatsApp booking updates when submitting your request. You can leave the box unticked and still submit your request.
