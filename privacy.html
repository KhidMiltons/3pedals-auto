Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 3 Pedals Auto KE (“3 Pedals”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you visit 3pedalsauto.co.ke, create an account, list or enquire about a vehicle, use garage or chauffeur services, or contact us.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, account type, password credentials handled by Supabase Auth, profile photo and seller/dealership profile information.
  • Date of birth: collected during account creation to enforce our minimum age of 13. The date is stored with account authentication metadata for age eligibility and account administration. We do not knowingly create or retain an account for a person under 13.
  • Marketplace information: vehicle details, prices, descriptions, photos, seller contact details, dealership information, saved vehicles, listing status, promotion history and seller-reported sale information.
  • Service information: garage and chauffeur booking details, vehicle and journey details, dates, locations, service progress, driver assignment, reviews and contact preferences.
  • Payment information: M-Pesa phone number, payment amount, payment status, receipt/reference identifiers and transaction timestamps. M-Pesa payment credentials and PINs are entered only in Safaricom's own prompt and are not collected by us.
  • Enquiries and communications: details you submit through contact forms, email, WhatsApp or other communication channels.
  • Technical information: basic request and security information processed by our hosting, database and security providers. Optional analytics and advertising data is only collected after you accept those optional technologies.

2. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify age eligibility, publish and manage vehicle listings, connect buyers and sellers, operate garage and chauffeur bookings, process one-time M-Pesa payments, send booking and listing notifications, respond to enquiries, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with legal obligations, and maintain and improve the service.

3. Optional analytics, advertising and cookies

Essential storage and processing support sign-in, account security, consent preferences and core site functions. Optional Google Tag Manager (including any analytics tags configured in that container) and the Meta Pixel are not requested or loaded until you select Accept optional cookies. Selecting Reject optional cookies keeps those optional tags disabled. Both choices are equally available, and you can reopen the choice using Cookie preferences in the site footer or Account Settings.

Our current homepage source audit did not identify a session-replay provider. We do not intentionally load session-replay software as part of this consent implementation. If we add such a provider, it must remain disabled until the user gives the required consent and this policy is updated.

4. Services and third parties that may process information

  • Supabase: account authentication, database, file/photo storage and serverless functions used to operate the marketplace and account features. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google: Google OAuth when you choose Google sign-in; Google Tag Manager and tags configured within it only after optional consent; Google Fonts for typography. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta: Meta Pixel and server-side Meta purchase conversion events only after you have accepted optional marketing tracking; WhatsApp Cloud API for service notifications where you have opted in; and social platforms if you choose to open our social links. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Safaricom: Daraja/M-Pesa for one-time promotion and chauffeur booking payments and payment callbacks. Safaricom.
  • Resend: transactional email delivery, including listing notifications and service emails. Resend Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: DNS services for the website domain.
  • GitHub Pages: static website hosting and delivery.
  • Truehost: domain registration for the website; it is not our website host.
  • jsDelivr: delivery of the Supabase JavaScript client library used by the site.
  • Unsplash: delivery of selected decorative and vehicle imagery. Your browser may connect to Unsplash to retrieve those images.
  • Vehicle catalogue data: the site includes a static vehicle make/model catalogue attributed to VehiclesDB. It is bundled with the website code; the current implementation does not send visitor personal information to VehiclesDB.

When you click an external link (including WhatsApp, email, social media or a payment prompt), the destination provider's own privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control third-party services or their independent processing. We do not sell personal information.

5. When we share information

We share only information needed to provide the service: seller contact details may be shown with a public listing; booking details may be shared with the assigned garage or chauffeur service provider; payment details required to initiate or reconcile a transaction are shared with Safaricom; and service providers listed above process information under their own terms. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users, or to address fraud, abuse or security incidents.

6. Public listings and user choices

Vehicle listing information and seller contact details you submit for publication may be visible to visitors. Do not include sensitive personal information in public listing descriptions or photos. Saved vehicles, account settings and private booking/payment details are intended to be restricted to the relevant account and authorised 3 Pedals administrators or service personnel who need them.

7. Retention and security

We retain account and transaction information for as long as needed to operate the account, complete services, resolve disputes, meet legal/accounting requirements and enforce our terms. Public listings may remain visible while approved and active. We use access controls, database row-level security, authentication and operational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children and minimum age

Accounts are available only to people aged 13 or older. Date of birth is checked before account creation and independently validated by the database. A rejected under-13 registration is not inserted into our authentication or profile tables. If you believe a child under 13 has somehow created an account or submitted personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it.

9. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw optional analytics/advertising consent. You can change optional cookie consent in Account Settings or the footer. To make a privacy request, email 3pedalsautoke@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

10. International processing

Some service providers may process information on infrastructure outside Kenya. Where this occurs, we use the providers' available contractual and security measures and take steps required by applicable data-protection law.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when services or legal requirements change. The latest effective date will appear at the top of this page. For privacy questions, contact 3pedalsautoke@gmail.com or visit 3pedalsauto.co.ke.
